## Why these specific 5?

Each article hits a different stage of the customer journey:

- **#04 (how to choose)**: Top of funnel. Customer doesn't know who to trust yet. Builds your brand authority.
- **#02 (Singaporean guide)**: Niche specificity. Captures "bangkok mobile massage singaporean" — almost no competition for this exact long-tail.
- **#01 (after-flight)** + **#05 (late-night)**: Pain-point capture. Customer is searching while feeling the problem.
- **#03 (business trip protocol)**: Authority + LinkedIn share-bait. Positions you as the executive choice, not the tourist choice.

## Publishing Plan (90 Days)

### Week 1
1. **Set up your blog.** If `sunred.app/blog` doesn't exist yet, build it. WordPress, Ghost, or even Notion published as a site works. Cost: 0-500 THB/month.
2. **Publish article #04** (how to choose) first. This is the trust-building piece. Takes pressure off you to "sell."

### Week 2
3. **Publish article #02** (Singaporean guide). Submit URL to Google Search Console.
4. **Cross-post abridged version (300-400 words) to Medium @sunred.** Link back to full article on your blog.

### Week 3
5. **Publish article #01** (after-flight).
6. **Adapt article #03** for LinkedIn — first-person, executive voice. Post under your founder account or a brand account. This typically performs well in business traveler networks.

### Week 4
7. **Publish article #03** in full on blog.
8. **Adapt article #05** as a Reddit r/Bangkok post (no direct sales pitch, just helpful info, with one link to the full article on your blog).

### Week 5-12
- Each week: Update one article with fresh data, internal links, or a small new section. Google rewards content freshness.
- Each week: Post 2-3 short helpful answers in expat forums (samsguide.living, Reddit, expat Singapore communities). Link back where appropriate.
- Track: Which articles bring traffic, which bring bookings, which bring inquiries.

## SEO Setup Checklist

For each article you publish:

- [ ] Title tag matches the article title (50-60 characters)
- [ ] Meta description set (140-155 characters) — use the `meta_description` field from the front matter
- [ ] URL slug matches the `slug` field from front matter
- [ ] H1 is the article title, used only once
- [ ] H2/H3 structure preserved from the markdown
- [ ] At least 2-3 internal links between your articles (Google rewards interconnected content)
- [ ] One outbound link to a credible source (e.g., a tourism authority page, a hotel listing) per article
- [ ] Schema.org Article markup added (most blog platforms do this automatically)
- [ ] Submit URL to Google Search Console after publishing

## What These Articles Will and Won't Do

**Will:**
- Bring organic traffic from Google in 60-90 days (initial), 4-6 months (compound)
- Rank you for niche long-tail queries with low competition
- Build trust and authority — customers find these and decide you're the professional choice
- Diversify you away from 100% dependency on samsguide.living

**Won't:**
- Bring traffic in week 1. SEO is slow. Don't measure week 1.
- Rank you for high-competition terms like "bangkok massage" — that's a long game and not the goal here.
- Convert at the same rate as paid ads. Organic SEO converts lower per visitor but at much lower cost. ROI compounds.

## Adapting These Articles

**Don't copy-paste verbatim across platforms.** Google penalizes duplicate content. Instead:

- **Your own blog**: Full article (1,000-1,500 words). The canonical version.
- **Medium**: Shortened version (400-600 words) with a "Read full article" link to your blog.
- **LinkedIn**: First-person adaptation, executive tone, 600-900 words. Drop the marketing call-to-action; LinkedIn rewards generosity.
- **Reddit**: Even shorter, conversational, no direct sales. Provide value, mention your service only if asked or natural.
- **Forums (samsguide.living etc.)**: Treat each forum's culture differently. Provide info-first comments. Build presence over weeks before any self-promotion.

## Internal Linking Strategy

After publishing all 5, link them like this:

- #01 → links to #02 (Singaporean readers want the full guide), #04 (how to choose)
- #02 → links to #04 (criteria), #05 (late-night), #03 (business protocol)
- #03 → links to #01 (recovery science), #02 (full guide)
- #04 → links to #02 (the guide it complements), #01 (the recovery context)
- #05 → links to #02 (full guide), #04 (how to choose)

This creates a tight content cluster that Google rewards strongly.

## Brand Voice Notes

The articles use a deliberately specific voice:

- Honest about the gray-area realities of the market without being explicit
- Quietly authoritative — written like an experienced traveler, not a marketer
- Uses comparison and specific numbers, not adjectives
- No exclamation marks, no emoji, no "amazing" / "incredible" / "transform your life"

This voice converts the Singaporean executive demographic. Loud or salesy copy actively repels them. Keep this voice consistent across all your content.
