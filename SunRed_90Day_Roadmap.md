# SunRed — 90-Day Solo Operator Roadmap

**Starting baseline (April 2026):**
- 25-30 bookings/month
- 100% revenue dependency on samsguide.living (15,000 THB/month ad spend)
- ~20 returning customers (memory often lost via Telegram chat clears)
- 8 active therapists
- Solo operator (you do everything)

**Goal (July 2026, end of 90 days):**
- 45-55 bookings/month
- ~50% revenue from samsguide.living, ~50% from diversified channels (SEO, referrals, repeat)
- 60+ logged customers in CRM
- 30%+ repeat rate from existing base
- 5+ hours/week saved through automation

---

## How to Use This Roadmap

This is a week-by-week plan written for a solo operator. Each week has:
- **🎯 Focus**: The one thing that matters most this week
- **✅ Tasks**: Specific actions, ordered by priority
- **⏱ Time**: Realistic time investment
- **💰 Cost**: New money spent (not counting the ongoing 15K/month samsguide spend)
- **📊 Success metric**: How to know it worked

**Rule for solo operators:** Don't try to do every task. If you only get 60% of a week done, move forward. Momentum > completionism.

## PHASE 1: STOP THE BLEEDING (Weeks 1-4)

### Week 1 (Apr 29 – May 5): Set Up Your Memory

🎯 **Focus:** Replace Telegram-dependent memory with a permanent CRM. This is the single highest-leverage move in the whole plan.

✅ **Tasks:**
1. Open `SunRed_CRM.xlsx` (delivered in your folder). Read the "How to Use" sheet first.
2. Spend 60-90 min logging every customer you remember from the past 6 months. Even partial info (nickname + hotel) is valuable.
3. Log every booking from the past 30 days into the Bookings sheet. This rebuilds your channel-ROI history.
4. Save the CRM to Google Drive or Dropbox so you can update it from your phone.
5. Make a personal commitment: log every new booking within 1 hour, no exceptions.

⏱ **Time:** 4-6 hours total this week

💰 **Cost:** 0 THB

📊 **Success metric:** CRM has at least 20 customer records and 30 booking records by Sunday night.

---

### Week 2 (May 6 – May 12): Install Quick-Reply System

🎯 **Focus:** Cut your message-reply time from 5 min to 30 seconds.

✅ **Tasks:**
1. Open `SunRed_Message_Templates.md`.
2. Install WhatsApp Business app (if not already). Free.
3. Set up Quick Replies in WhatsApp Business — at minimum: `/welcome`, `/price`, `/confirm`, `/firsttime`, `/discreet`.
4. Same for Telegram via saved messages.
5. Use the templates for every inquiry this week. Edit `[BRACKETS]` before sending. Don't send raw templates.
6. **Track reply speed:** measure how fast you respond to the first 10 inquiries this week. Goal: 80% within 5 minutes.

⏱ **Time:** 2-3 hours setup + ongoing daily use

💰 **Cost:** 0 THB

📊 **Success metric:** Average reply speed under 5 minutes. Inquiry-to-booking conversion rate up 10-20%.

---

### Week 3 (May 13 – May 19): Re-Engage Your Existing Customers

🎯 **Focus:** Your 20 existing customers are gold. Most haven't been contacted recently. Wake them up.

✅ **Tasks:**
1. Filter CRM Customers sheet by "Last Visit" — any customer 60+ days ago = re-engagement target.
2. Send each of them a personal message using template **5.1** (60-90 day) or **5.2** (90+ day).
3. **Critical:** Personalize 1-2 sentences for each. Reference their hotel, their preferred therapist, anything specific. Generic messages get ignored.
4. Don't bulk-send. Send 5-10 per day across the week to feel personal.
5. Log every reply in the CRM Notes column.

⏱ **Time:** 30-45 min/day for 5 days

💰 **Cost:** 0 THB (no ads, just messaging)

📊 **Success metric:** 3-5 re-bookings from this batch. At 2,500-3,000 THB each = 10,000-15,000 THB recovered revenue. ROI on this single week = infinite.

---

### Week 4 (May 20 – May 26): Set Up the Blog

🎯 **Focus:** Build the foundation for organic traffic. SEO is slow — start now.

✅ **Tasks:**
1. Buy a domain if you don't have a blog URL (sunred.app/blog or similar). ~500 THB/year.
2. Set up the blog. Easiest options: WordPress (~300 THB/month for hosting), Ghost (~$9 USD/month), or Notion site (free, simple).
3. Open `SEO_Blog_Pack/` folder. Read the README publishing strategy file.
4. **Publish article #04** first ("How to Choose a Mobile Massage Service in Bangkok"). This is the trust-builder.
5. Submit your blog URL to Google Search Console. Free.
6. Add internal links: from your main site/booking page, link to the blog. From the blog, link to your booking WhatsApp.

⏱ **Time:** 4-6 hours total

💰 **Cost:** ~500-1,000 THB for domain + first month hosting

📊 **Success metric:** Blog is live, 1 article published, Search Console verified.

## PHASE 2: DIVERSIFY ACQUISITION (Weeks 5-8)

### Week 5 (May 27 – Jun 2): Publish More + A/B Test Ads

🎯 **Focus:** Two channels in parallel — content compounding + ad testing.

✅ **Tasks:**
1. Publish article #02 (Singaporean Guide). Submit to Search Console.
2. Cross-post abridged version (400-500 words) to Medium under @sunred. Link back to full article.
3. Open `SunRed_Ad_Copy_Bank.md`. Pick two angles to test on samsguide.living: **ANGLE 1 (Late-Flight)** vs **ANGLE 5 (First-Time)**.
4. Run them simultaneously for 2 weeks. Track inquiries each ad generates by including a small phrase in each (e.g., "saw your post about late flights" vs "first time in Bangkok").

⏱ **Time:** 4-5 hours

💰 **Cost:** Same 15K/month samsguide spend, just split between two ads

📊 **Success metric:** Each ad gets at least 3 attributed inquiries by end of week.

---

### Week 6 (Jun 3 – Jun 9): Hotel Concierge Outreach

🎯 **Focus:** Open the hotel-concierge channel. Slow to build, but durable once it works.

✅ **Tasks:**
1. List 10 hotels in Sukhumvit Soi 1-25 where your customers stay most. Use CRM data to identify.
2. Use template **7.1** to send WhatsApp/email to 5 of them this week, 5 next week.
3. **Don't send identical messages.** Personalize each — mention specific hotel, specific reason ("we already serve your guests regularly").
4. Goal: 2 concierge meetings (15-min coffees) booked.
5. Publish article #01 (After-Flight Recovery) on blog and Medium.

⏱ **Time:** 6-8 hours

💰 **Cost:** ~500 THB for coffee meetings

📊 **Success metric:** 1-2 concierge meetings agreed. 1 article more published.

---

### Week 7 (Jun 10 – Jun 16): Reddit / Forum Presence

🎯 **Focus:** Start a low-key forum presence. Volume of helpful posts > volume of promotional ones.

✅ **Tasks:**
1. Create / reuse Reddit account. Post 3-5 helpful comments on r/Bangkok, r/ThailandTourism, r/singapore answering massage-related questions. **No self-promotion in any of these.** Just genuinely helpful.
2. After 1 week of helpful posts, your account has standing. Then publish article #04 as a Reddit post on r/Bangkok with a one-sentence intro: "Wrote up the criteria I use for evaluating Bangkok mobile services after years of living here. Hope it's useful."
3. Adapt **ANGLE 11 (Insider Rec)** as a forum post for samsguide.living. Test if it outperforms the polished ads.
4. Publish article #03 (Business Trip Recovery) on blog. Adapt for LinkedIn.

⏱ **Time:** 5-7 hours

💰 **Cost:** 0 THB

📊 **Success metric:** Reddit account has karma + 1-2 inquiries cite "saw on Reddit." LinkedIn post gets 50+ views.

### Week 8 (Jun 17 – Jun 23): Concierge Meetings + Repeat Loop

🎯 **Focus:** Close concierge deals + lock down repeat customer process.

✅ **Tasks:**
1. Hold 2 concierge meetings booked from week 6. Pitch: 20% commission on referred bookings, monthly settlement, plain-clothes professional therapists, late-night availability.
2. For every booking this week, use template **4.1** (post-session thank-you with soft review ask). Get 2-3 Google reviews.
3. Mid-week: review CRM. Any "Inactive 60+" customers? Send template **5.1**. Any "VIP" customers? Send template **4.3** with a small thank-you.
4. Publish article #05 (Late-Night Options).

⏱ **Time:** 6-8 hours

💰 **Cost:** Coffee meeting cost (~500 THB)

📊 **Success metric:** 1 concierge partnership signed. 5+ articles total live. 2-3 new Google reviews.

## PHASE 3: COMPOUND + AUTOMATE (Weeks 9-12)

### Week 9 (Jun 24 – Jun 30): Automate the Busywork

🎯 **Focus:** Stop doing things a script can do.

✅ **Tasks:**
1. Sign up for Make.com (free tier handles ~500 ops/month, plenty for you).
2. Build automation #1: New booking entered in CRM → auto-send WhatsApp confirmation to customer using template **2.1**. Saves 5 min per booking = ~3 hours/month.
3. Build automation #2: Customer inactive 60 days → auto-create reminder for you to send re-engagement message. Doesn't auto-send (needs personalization), but flags it.
4. Build automation #3: Daily summary of new bookings to your email at 9am. Helps you start each day with a clear picture.
5. Document each automation in a Notion page so you can troubleshoot later.

⏱ **Time:** 4-6 hours setup, then 0 ongoing

💰 **Cost:** 0 THB (free Make.com tier sufficient)

📊 **Success metric:** 3 working automations. Confirmed 5+ hours saved/week.

---

### Week 10 (Jul 1 – Jul 7): Data Review + Channel Decisions

🎯 **Focus:** Look at the data. Cut what's failing. Double down on what's working.

✅ **Tasks:**
1. Open CRM "Ad Channel ROI" sheet. Review every channel's performance over last 30-60 days.
2. **samsguide.living analysis:** If it's still your top channel by booking count, keep. If it's <30% of bookings, consider dropping budget by 50% and using the saved money on:
   - A second SG-focused ad placement (test, don't commit)
   - More blog content (~5,000 THB/article if outsourced; or your time if not)
   - Hotel concierge commission pool
3. Look at "Top 10 Customers" on Dashboard. These are 60-70% of your revenue. Send each a personal message with a small thank-you offer.
4. Publish 1-2 new articles for the blog (use ChatGPT to draft, you edit). Topic ideas: "Bangkok Hotel Concierge Guide for SG Travelers," "Recovery Routines for SG Marathon Runners in Bangkok."

⏱ **Time:** 6-8 hours

💰 **Cost:** 0 THB (or up to 5,000 THB if outsourcing content)

📊 **Success metric:** Decision made on samsguide budget. Top-10 customers messaged. 1-2 new articles published.

---

### Week 11 (Jul 8 – Jul 14): Expand Therapist Capacity OR Improve Existing

🎯 **Focus:** Bottleneck identification. The main growth constraint at 45-55 bookings/month is usually *which therapist customers want vs. who's available.*

✅ **Tasks:**
1. Open CRM "Therapists" sheet. Look at "Total Bookings" column.
2. **Top 2-3 therapists** are your driver. Make sure they're happy — pay on time, fair commission, prioritize them with VIP customers.
3. **Bottom therapists** with <3 bookings: have an honest conversation. Either coach them up, restructure their hours, or wind down.
4. **Hire 1-2 new therapists** if your top therapists are turning away bookings due to availability. Use vetted recruitment channels (referral from existing therapists is most reliable).
5. Update CRM Therapists sheet with any changes.

⏱ **Time:** 5-8 hours (more if interviewing)

💰 **Cost:** 0 THB direct (commission shifts)

📊 **Success metric:** Clear top-3 therapists prioritized. Booking-decline rate down. 1 new therapist onboarded if needed.

### Week 12 (Jul 15 – Jul 21): Review + Plan the Next 90 Days

🎯 **Focus:** Step back. Look at where you are vs. where you started. Decide what's next.

✅ **Tasks:**
1. **Numbers review:** Bookings/month now vs. April. Channel mix now vs. April. Hours/week worked now vs. April. Revenue now vs. April.
2. **Customer review:** How many in CRM? How many VIP? How many Inactive? Send VIPs another personal message — they're the foundation of the next quarter.
3. **What worked:** Which 2-3 actions from Weeks 1-11 created the biggest impact? Do more of those next quarter.
4. **What didn't work:** Which 2-3 actions were time sinks with no return? Stop doing them.
5. **90-day plan v2:** Based on the data, plan the next quarter. Major decisions:
   - Keep samsguide.living at 15K? Reduce? Add a second channel?
   - Hire someone part-time to handle messaging? (3,000-5,000 THB/week pays back if you're capacity-bound)
   - Build the SunRed app booking flow more aggressively?
   - Add new service categories?

⏱ **Time:** 4-6 hours of strategic thinking

💰 **Cost:** 0 THB

📊 **Success metric:** Clear written plan for next 90 days. At least 3 specific decisions made.

## What to Do If Things Go Sideways

**If samsguide.living traffic dries up suddenly:**
- Don't panic. Phase 1 work (CRM + repeat customer engagement) can sustain 50-70% of bookings short-term.
- Accelerate Phase 2 (hotel concierge + Reddit) — these are slower but durable.

**If you hit a busy stretch and can't follow this plan:**
- Pick one task per week minimum: the CRM update.
- Skip blog publishing for 1-2 weeks — content compounds, you can catch up.
- Never skip the message templates (use them every day).

**If a competitor copies your blog content:**
- Common in SEO. Google rewards the original publisher; your dates and Search Console submissions protect you.
- Keep publishing. Volume + freshness + internal linking wins long-term.

**If a therapist quits:**
- Common in this industry. CRM Therapists sheet shows you who's redundant vs. critical.
- Have one backup therapist relationship per service area.

## Final Note from Me

You're a solo operator running a niche service in a tough market with real constraints. This roadmap is realistic, not aspirational. Every week's tasks are achievable in 5-8 hours of focused work.

The single biggest mistake at this stage of the business is doing too many things half-finished. **Pick the one task each week that matters most, finish it, then move on.**

The CRM (Week 1) is the highest-leverage thing in the entire plan. If you only do that one thing in the next 90 days, you'll still see meaningful improvement in repeat-customer revenue. Everything else compounds on top.

Good luck. You've got this.
