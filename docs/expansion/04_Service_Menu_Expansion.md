## 1. COUPLES PACKAGE (TWO-THERAPIST SAME-ROOM)

**Target Segment:** Couple Travelers (anniversary, honeymoon, special occasion)

**What it is:** Two therapists, one room, parallel sessions. Both clients receive their massage simultaneously, can talk during the session if they want.

**Service variants:**
- **Couples Aroma 90min** — 2 therapists × 90 min = 4,500 THB
- **Couples Deep Tissue 90min** — 2 therapists × 90 min = 4,800 THB
- **Couples Anniversary Premium** — 2 therapists × 120 min + rose petals on table + champagne note = 6,000 THB

**Operational requirements:**
- 2 therapists who work well together (rehearse the parallel timing once)
- Larger hotel rooms or suites (requires 4×4m space minimum)
- Slightly longer setup (15 min vs. 10 min for solo)
- Pre-confirm room is suitable when booking

**Margin:** Same therapist commission percentage, but you keep operator margin on both. Average net margin: ~25-30% better than two separate solo bookings.

**Where to market:**
- TripAdvisor "things to do for couples in Bangkok"
- Hotel concierge (couples ask concierges most)
- SEO content: "Anniversary trip to Bangkok ideas"
- Wedding planners and honeymoon agents

**Pitch line:** *"For your anniversary in Bangkok — two therapists, in your suite, at the time you choose. We coordinate the timing so you finish together."*

## 2. BANGKOK EXPAT MEMBERSHIP (4-PACK)

**Target Segment:** Bangkok Expats (recurring monthly customers)

**What it is:** Pre-paid 4-session pack at 10% discount, valid 4 months, with same-therapist guarantee.

**Pricing structure:**
- **4× Aroma 60min** = 6,500 THB (vs. 7,200 retail = save 700)
- **4× Aroma 90min** = 9,000 THB (vs. 10,000 retail = save 1,000)
- **4× Deep Tissue 90min** = 10,000 THB (vs. 11,200 retail = save 1,200)

**The genius of this package:**
- Customer commits to 4 sessions upfront — your cash flow improves
- Same-therapist guarantee creates retention (they can't skip — they've paid)
- 4-month expiry creates urgency
- Average expat books 8-12 times/year anyway — discount is on bookings they were going to make

**Operational requirements:**
- Track membership credits in CRM (add column "Pack Credits Remaining")
- Therapist availability lock (book all 4 dates upfront, or at least the first one)
- Refund policy: non-refundable but transferable to spouse/partner

**Margin:** 10% discount on 4 sessions = customer saves ~1,000 THB. You gain: locked-in revenue, lower acquisition cost (reuse customer), reduced friction. Net positive.

**Where to market:**
- Bangkok Expat Telegram groups (silently first; pack offer when trust is built)
- Bangkok Expat Facebook groups
- LinkedIn Bangkok professional network
- Word of mouth from current expat customers (offer 500 THB referral credit)

**Pitch line:** *"4 sessions, same therapist, your schedule. Pre-paid means we lock your slot. 10% off retail."*

## 8. HOTEL CONCIERGE PRIVATE LINE

**Target Segment:** All segments via hotel concierge channel

**What it is:** Not a service exactly — a B2B offering. Concierges of partner hotels get a dedicated WhatsApp number and priority dispatch.

**Pricing:**
- Same retail prices to customer
- 25% commission to concierge (settled monthly)
- Free for the hotel — no setup cost

**Why it works:**
- Removes friction for concierge to recommend you
- Concierge gets paid monthly, no awkward cash transactions
- You get high-LTV traveler customers (concierges send their best customers)

**Operational requirements:**
- One additional WhatsApp number for concierge bookings (or tag bookings in CRM)
- Monthly commission tracking and payment
- Print 500 small business cards with concierge contact info (1,500 THB one-time)

**Where to market:**
- Direct outreach to 20-30 hotel concierges (template in main message file)
- Coffee meetings to build relationships
- Hotel General Managers if you want to formalize

- Reviews on Klook = organic discovery for future bookings

- Photos for listing (use generic ambient photos — same approach as your blog)
- Cancellation policy must match Klook's standards
- Process bookings through Klook's vendor dashboard

**Where to market:**
- The platform itself does the marketing
- Just optimize the listing well

## Final Note: Don't Build New Services. Build New Frames.

Every offering above uses your existing therapists, existing massage techniques, and existing booking flow. The differentiation is **packaging, positioning, and channel** — not new physical capabilities.

This is the leverage of a solo operator: the same body of work can be monetized through multiple framings, each unlocking a different segment. Your therapist gives the same Aroma 90min whether the customer is an SG businessman, a Bangkok expat, or a Marathon runner — but the customer experiences (and pays for) something different in each case.

That's the entire game.
