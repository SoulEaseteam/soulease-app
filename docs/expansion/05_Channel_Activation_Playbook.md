# SunRed — Channel Activation Playbook

**Purpose:** Step-by-step setup for each new acquisition channel. Each section answers: what is this channel, how to set it up, what it costs, what to post, expected timeline, and how to know it's working.

**Use this doc:** Pick ONE channel per week. Don't try to set up multiple at once. Each takes ~3-6 hours to launch properly.

---

## CHANNEL 1: HOTEL CONCIERGE NETWORK

**Why this is your #1 channel to build:** Concierges send their best customers. High-LTV. Works for 8 segments simultaneously (SG, HK, JP, KR, ME, Couples, Female, Medical).

### Setup (Week 1, ~6 hours)

**Step 1: Build the target list**
- Identify 20 hotels where your customers stay (use CRM data)
- Add 10 more hotels in Sukhumvit Soi 1-25, Silom/Sathorn, Riverside that you don't yet have customers from
- For each hotel, find concierge name (LinkedIn or hotel website)

**Step 2: Print materials**
- 500 small business cards: brand name, WhatsApp/Telegram number, "Mobile massage to your guests' room — 25% concierge commission" (in English)
- Cost: ~1,500 THB at SiamSquare or any printer

**Step 3: First contact (per concierge)**
- Stop by the hotel mid-morning (10-11am, lobbies are quiet)
- Ask for the concierge by name
- 2-minute pitch in English: "I run a mobile massage service that primarily serves your guest profile. We pay 25% commission on every booking we receive through you. Here's a card. Can I send you our therapist roster on WhatsApp?"
- Don't push for immediate commitment
- Get their WhatsApp

**Step 4: Follow-up (within 24 hours)**
- Send the WhatsApp message using template **7.1** from the Multi-Language Templates
- Attach 3 photos: clean hotel-room massage setup (no faces), oils/towels, branded business card
- Wait 3-5 days, then send template **7.2** if no reply

**Step 5: Activation incentive**
- Offer a complimentary 60-min session to the concierge themselves (real people respond to real value)
- Cost to you: ~800 THB therapist commission. Return: a concierge who's experienced your service first-hand.

### Cost
- Cards: 1,500 THB one-time
- Coffee meetings: 1,000-2,000 THB total
- Complimentary sessions for concierges: 4,000-8,000 THB across 5-10 partners
- **Total Phase 1 investment: ~6,000-12,000 THB**

### Timeline
- Week 1: First 10 contacts + cards distributed
- Week 2-3: Follow-ups + 2-3 partnerships agreed
- Week 4-8: First referred bookings start (slow)
- Month 3-6: 10-30 referred bookings/month at scale

### Success metrics
- 3+ active concierge partnerships by Day 30
- 5-10 referred bookings/month by Day 90
- Average referred booking value 30-50% higher than direct (concierges send premium guests)

## CHANNEL 2: KLOOK / VIATOR MARKETPLACE

**Why this matters:** Tourists from CN, TW, KR, JP, ID who don't trust direct chat with a foreign service. Klook = trust layer. Pre-paid, high volume.

### Setup (Week 2-3, ~8 hours total spread over 2-4 weeks waiting period)

**Step 1: Apply as a vendor**
- Klook Partner Hub: partner.klook.com
- Required: business registration, 1+ year operating history, English description, 5+ photos
- Application takes 10-15 days for review

**Step 2: Build the listing**
- Title: "Bangkok In-Room Hotel Massage — Aroma 90min" (specific, searchable)
- Description: 200-400 words, focused on "what guests experience" not feature list
- Photos: 6-10 ambient hotel/oil/towel photos (no faces). Use Adobe Firefly to generate if needed.
- Pricing: Klook commission 20-25%. Set retail at 3,200 THB so you net ~2,400-2,500 THB after commission.
- Cancellation policy: must match Klook standard (free cancellation 24hr+ prior)

**Step 3: Operational setup**
- Klook bookings come through their vendor dashboard
- Set up daily check-in routine: log in 2x/day to see new bookings
- Confirm bookings within 4 hours (Klook penalizes slow vendors)
- Check Klook "Performance" tab weekly

**Step 4: First reviews are critical**
- For first 5-10 Klook bookings: over-deliver. Slightly longer session, higher-quality oil, perfect timing.
- After session, message customer politely asking for review on Klook (not on your direct channels)
- Reach 4.7+ star rating before scaling marketing — below this you won't appear in search.

### Cost
- Klook listing: free
- Commission: 20-25% per booking (built into your pricing)
- Photo creation: 0-2,000 THB if you use AI tools

### Timeline
- Week 2: Submit application
- Week 3-4: Build listing
- Week 4-5: Listing goes live
- Week 6-12: 5-15 bookings/month

### Success metrics
- Listing live with 5+ photos by Week 4
- First Klook booking by Week 6
- 4.7+ rating by Week 12
- 10+ bookings/month by Month 4

### Repeat for Viator
- Same process at viator.com/partner
- Slightly higher commission (25-30%) but reaches Western tourists Klook misses
- Worth doing both for full coverage

## CHANNEL 3: WECHAT (MAINLAND CHINA SETUP)

**Why bother:** Mainland Chinese are 30% of Bangkok visitor volume. They don't use WhatsApp/Telegram — WeChat is mandatory. High spending segment.

### Setup (Week 4-6, ~10 hours + VA help)

**Step 1: WeChat Official Account**
- Type: Service Account (not Subscription) — allows direct messaging
- Verification: requires Chinese business entity. Workaround: use a third-party verification service (~$300-500 USD one-time) to verify under a Hong Kong / Singapore entity. Search "WeChat Official Account verification overseas business."

**Step 2: WeChat Mini Program (optional but powerful)**
- Allows in-WeChat booking flow
- Setup cost: $500-1,500 USD for a basic version via Fiverr
- Skip in Phase 1; come back to this in Month 4-5 when WeChat traffic justifies it

**Step 3: Hire a Chinese-speaking VA (one-time setup)**
- Fiverr or Upwork: $50-100 USD
- Tasks: register WeChat account, write Chinese welcome message, build basic auto-reply flow with Mandarin templates from your Multi-Language doc
- 6-8 hours of VA work total

**Step 4: Xiaohongshu (小红书) presence**
- Free app, no business verification needed for personal account
- Post 3-5 visual content pieces per week
- Content type: ambient hotel/oils/towels photos (no faces) with Mandarin captions
- Use Claude/ChatGPT to translate captions
- Hashtags: #曼谷按摩 #曼谷酒店 #曼谷旅行

**Step 5: WeChat Pay for payment**
- Set up via WeChat Pay merchant (third-party services do this for non-Chinese businesses for ~$200-500 USD setup)
- Phase 1 alternative: customers pay via Alipay or cash on arrival. Less ideal but works.

### Cost
- VA setup: ~$50-100 USD (~1,800-3,500 THB)
- WeChat verification: ~$300-500 USD (~10,000-17,000 THB) — only if you go full WeChat Official Account
- Xiaohongshu: free
- **Phase 1 minimum (Xiaohongshu only): 3,500 THB**
- **Phase 2 (full WeChat): +17,000 THB**

### Timeline
- Week 4: Xiaohongshu account live, first 5 posts
- Week 5-6: WeChat verification submitted
- Week 8-10: WeChat Official Account live
- Month 3-4: First Mandarin bookings

### Success metrics
- Xiaohongshu: 100+ followers by Week 12
- WeChat: 50+ added contacts by Month 4
- 2-5 Mandarin-speaking bookings/month by Month 4

### Honest assessment
- WeChat is high-effort, slow-payoff. Don't prioritize unless Phase 1 channels (concierge, Klook) are saturated.
- Xiaohongshu alone is enough to test the Chinese market without WeChat infrastructure.

## CHANNEL 4: BANGKOK EXPAT TELEGRAM / FACEBOOK GROUPS

**Why this is highest-LTV channel:** Bangkok expats book 12-24 times/year. One acquired expat = 8-12 acquired travelers in lifetime value.

### Setup (Week 1-2, ~4 hours setup, ongoing presence)

**Step 1: Identify groups (free reconnaissance)**
- **Telegram groups to find and join:**
  - "Bangkok Expats" (largest, ~50K members)
  - "Sukhumvit Expats"
  - "Thonglor Expats"
  - "BKK Pro" (professionals)
  - "Bangkok Newcomers"
  - "Bangkok Wellness"
  - "Bangkok Business Network"
- **Facebook groups:**
  - "Bangkok Expats" (FB)
  - "Bangkok Foreign Wives Network" (relevant for couples bookings)
  - "Bangkok Health & Wellness"

**Step 2: Build group standing FIRST (Weeks 1-3)**
- Lurk for the first week — observe tone, etiquette, what gets engagement, what gets banned
- Week 2: post 3-5 helpful answers to massage/wellness/Bangkok-life questions. **No self-promotion.**
- Week 3: Continue helpful posts. Add Bangkok food, transit, services answers — anything useful.

**Step 3: Self-disclosure (Week 4+)**
- Once you have group standing, you can disclose. Suggested format:
  > "Full disclosure — I run a small mobile massage service in Bangkok (SunRed), so take my views with that context. That said, [your helpful answer]. I'd rather you book the right service than the cheap one."
- This works because you're providing value, not pitching.

**Step 4: Specific posts that drive bookings**
- Once a month, you can post a value-first share. Examples:
  - "I work with mobile massage and notice many expats here struggle to find a reliable monthly maintenance therapist. Here's what to look for: [criteria]. Happy to share more if useful."
  - Don't post links. Don't tag yourself. People who want to find you will DM.

### Cost
- Free entirely
- Time: 2-3 hours/week of presence, declining to 30-60 min/week after Month 2

### Timeline
- Week 1-3: Build standing (no booking impact)
- Week 4-8: First DMs from group members (1-3 inquiries/week)
- Month 3-6: 5-15 acquired expat customers
- Year 1: 20-40 expat customers (most of them VIPs)

### Success metrics
- 5+ helpful posts contributed by Week 4
- 1+ inquiry/week from groups by Week 6
- 3-5 booked Bangkok Expat customers by Month 3

### Critical rules
- **Never spam.** One self-disclosure post per group per month max.
- **Never use multiple accounts.** Group admins can tell. You'll get banned permanently.
- **Always provide value first.** A useful post about Bangkok dentists or visa runs builds more goodwill than 10 promotional posts.

## CHANNEL 5: LINE OFFICIAL ACCOUNT (THAI LOCAL MARKET)

**Why:** Thai locals don't use WhatsApp. LINE is dominant. LINE OA is free for basic tier.

### Setup (Week 6, ~3 hours)

**Step 1: Create LINE OA**
- Go to: lineforbusiness.com (Thai)
- Sign up for free Light tier (up to 200 friends, unlimited messages)
- Upload logo (use your existing SunRed logo)
- Profile description: in Thai — "บริการนวดถึงโรงแรม / ที่พัก กรุงเทพฯ จองง่าย ราคาชัดเจน" (Mobile massage to hotel/residence in Bangkok — easy booking, transparent pricing)

**Step 2: Set up auto-reply (greeting message)**
- Use Thai template from Multi-Language doc Section 1
- LINE OA backend → "Auto-reply" → set greeting

**Step 3: Add basic rich menu**
- Free in LINE OA
- 4-6 buttons: Services, Pricing, Book Now, About, Reviews
- Each button leads to a pre-set message reply

**Step 4: Get LINE QR code**
- Print on cards
- Add to your existing website / SEO articles
- Add to gift voucher (Thai Local Gift Voucher offering)

**Step 5: Promote selectively**
- Post in Thai-language Facebook groups: "Bangkok Wellness," "Thai Health & Beauty"
- Reach out to Thai concierge in office buildings
- Don't try to compete on price with cheap local mobile services

### Cost
- LINE OA Light tier: free
- Rich menu design: free (use Canva)
- LINE Ads (optional): start with 1,000-2,000 THB/month if you want to test paid acquisition

### Timeline
- Week 6: Account live
- Week 7-12: Slow growth, first 10-30 friends
- Month 3-6: First 5-10 Thai bookings

### Success metrics
- LINE OA live with rich menu by Week 6
- 50+ friends by Month 3
- 1-3 Thai bookings/month by Month 4

### Realistic expectations
- Thai market is slow to convert. Don't over-invest.
- The gift voucher angle (Service Menu Expansion #6) is your best Thai growth lever.
- Focus is **premium positioning, not volume.**

## CHANNEL 6: TRIPADVISOR LISTING

**Why:** Free. International travelers research on TripAdvisor before booking. Reviews compound.

### Setup (Week 2, ~2 hours)

**Step 1: List your business**
- Go to: tripadvisor.com/Owners
- Add SunRed as "Spa & Wellness" in Bangkok
- Required: business name, address (your registered address), description, photos
- Verification: takes 5-7 days

**Step 2: Optimize listing**
- Description focuses on "mobile to hotel rooms," "international travelers," "professional service"
- 5+ ambient photos (no faces)
- Pricing range listed clearly
- Languages: English, Mandarin, Japanese (whichever you support)

**Step 3: Get first 10 reviews**
- After every booking with international customers, ask: "If you have a moment, a review on TripAdvisor really helps our small team."
- Make it easy: send the direct link via WhatsApp post-session
- Don't pay for reviews. Don't fake reviews. TripAdvisor catches both.

**Step 4: Maintain rating**
- Respond to every review (positive or negative) professionally and warmly
- Negative review? Acknowledge, apologize, offer to make it right. Don't argue.

### Cost
- Free
- Time: 30 min/week ongoing

### Timeline
- Week 2-3: Listing live
- Month 1-2: First 5 reviews
- Month 3-6: 15-30 reviews
- Month 6+: TripAdvisor becomes a trust signal that closes hesitant inquiries

### Success metrics
- Listing live by Week 3
- 10+ reviews by Month 3
- 4.5+ stars maintained
- 5-15 TripAdvisor-attributed bookings/month by Month 6

## CHANNEL 7: REDDIT (R/BANGKOK, R/THAILANDTOURISM)

**Why:** High-quality international travelers. English-speaking. Critical for HK, AUS, EU, US, MY segments. Free.

### Setup (Week 3, ongoing)

**Step 1: Build account standing**
- Use existing Reddit account (or create one with at least some history)
- New accounts get auto-flagged as spam in r/Bangkok
- If you must use a new account, build 100+ karma in unrelated subreddits first (cooking, travel photography, etc.)

**Step 2: Post helpful content (Weeks 3-6)**
- Answer 5-10 Bangkok travel questions per week
- Don't mention SunRed at all in this period
- Topics: visa, food, transport, hotels, neighborhoods, weather, scams to avoid

**Step 3: Strategic share (Week 7+)**
- Post your SEO blog article #04 ("How to Choose a Mobile Massage Service in Bangkok") as a Reddit post
- Headline: "After years living in Bangkok, here's how I evaluate mobile massage services" (more authentic than "I wrote a blog post")
- Add a one-line disclosure: "Full disclosure: I run a small service called SunRed. The criteria here apply to evaluating us, our competitors, or anyone else."
- Don't link to your service. Mention only on direct DM if asked.

**Step 4: Recurring presence**
- 1 helpful post + 5-10 helpful comments per week
- Quarterly: one major content share (a blog post adapted for Reddit)

### Cost
- Free
- Time: 1-2 hours/week

### Timeline
- Week 3-6: Build standing
- Week 7+: First strategic post
- Month 2-3: First Reddit-attributed bookings (1-3/month)
- Year 1: Steady 3-8 bookings/month from Reddit + ongoing brand authority

### Success metrics
- 100+ karma in r/Bangkok by Week 8
- 1+ post performs well (50+ upvotes) by Week 12
- 3+ Reddit-attributed bookings/month by Month 6

### Critical rules
- **Read each subreddit's rules before posting.** r/Bangkok bans most service self-promotion.
- **Be a real person.** Don't write like a marketing brochure. Use casual punctuation, occasional humor, admit uncertainty.

## CHANNEL 9: STRAVA / RUNNING CLUBS (MARATHON SEGMENT)

**Why:** Bangkok Marathon (November) brings 5,000+ international runners. Specific-day demand spike. Premium positioning.

### Setup (Week 8-10, before November race)

**Step 1: Identify clubs**
- Strava: search clubs with "Bangkok" + "marathon" — find Singapore/HK clubs whose members run Bangkok races
- Facebook: "Bangkok Runners Club," "Singapore Marathon Group"

**Step 2: Direct outreach to club admins**
- WhatsApp/Telegram club admins
- Pitch: "I run a mobile massage service in Bangkok specifically catering to marathon recovery. Would your members find a 10% group discount useful for the November race?"
- Offer: 10% off Marathon Recovery Pack for verified club members

**Step 3: Race-week presence**
- 4 weeks before Bangkok Marathon: post in clubs about pre-race recovery tips
- 1 week before: announce your Marathon Pack with race-specific availability
- Race weekend: dedicated dispatch capacity (you may need to coordinate 2-3 therapists for the demand spike)

**Step 4: Repeat for other races**
- Same process for Khon Kaen Marathon (January), Bangkok Triathlon (varies), Songkran Sunset Run

### Cost
- 10% group discount: ~250-300 THB per booking
- Free outreach time

### Timeline
- Week 8: Identify clubs
- Week 9-10: Initial outreach
- Race weekend: 5-15 marathon bookings in 3 days

### Success metrics
- 3+ club partnerships by race weekend
- 10+ marathon-pack bookings during Bangkok Marathon weekend
- 1-2 long-term repeat customers from each marathon (runners travel for multiple races)

## CHANNEL PRIORITIZATION (Given 5-15K THB/Month Budget)

If you can only do 4 channels in 90 days, do these in order:

| Priority | Channel | Why First | Phase 1 Cost |
|----------|---------|-----------|--------------|
| 1 | Hotel Concierge | Highest LTV, multi-segment | 6,000-12,000 THB |
| 2 | Bangkok Expat Telegram/Facebook | Free + highest LTV segment | 0 THB |
| 3 | Klook + TripAdvisor | Free volume from tourists | 0 THB (commission only) |

Phase 2 (Months 4-6, after Phase 1 generates real data):
- WeChat / Xiaohongshu (CN segment)
- LINE OA (Thai segment)
- HK forums (LIHKG)

## KEY DECISIONS YOU'LL FACE

**"Should I run paid ads on a new channel?"**
- Only after organic posts on that channel show genuine engagement
- Test budget: never start above 3,000 THB/month per new channel
- If 3 months at 3,000 THB doesn't generate >9,000 THB in attributed revenue, cut

**"Should I respond to every single inquiry?"**
- Yes — speed of reply is your #1 conversion lever
- Use AI translation for languages you don't speak
- Use templates for routine replies (saves 80% of typing time)

**"Should I drop samsguide.living?"**
- Not in Phase 1
- Reduce when alternative channels generate >40% of bookings
- The risk of dropping early is going from 30 bookings/mo back to 10

**"How do I track which channel brought which booking?"**
- CRM "Acquisition Channel" column (already added in v2)
- Ask in first message: "How did you find SunRed?"
- For paid channels, use unique discount codes (e.g., REDDIT2026, KLOOK2026) so attribution is automatic

## FINAL CHECKLIST BEFORE YOU START

Before activating ANY new channel, verify:

- [ ] Your CRM v2 is set up and you're logging every booking
- [ ] Your message templates are saved as Quick Replies in WhatsApp Business
- [ ] You can reply to inquiries within 5 minutes during waking hours
- [ ] You have at least 2-3 ambient brand photos ready (no faces)
- [ ] Your existing samsguide.living service quality is consistent
- [ ] You've decided which 1-2 service expansions you'll lead with (Couples? Bangkok Expat 4-pack?)

Once these are in place, channel activation is straightforward execution. The infrastructure is what makes the channels work — not the other way around.
