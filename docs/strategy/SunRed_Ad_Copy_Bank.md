## A/B Testing Framework

### How to test these systematically

You're spending 15,000 THB/month on samsguide.living. Don't run one ad. Run two at a time.

**Week 1-2:** Run ANGLE 1 (Late-Flight) vs ANGLE 5 (First-Time)
**Week 3-4:** Winner of W1-2 vs ANGLE 3 (Discreet Professional)
**Week 5-6:** Winner of W3-4 vs ANGLE 11 (Insider Rec - forum-native)
**Week 7-8:** Winner of W5-6 vs ANGLE 6 (Weekend Package)

By Week 8, you'll know which 1-2 angles convert best for your specific audience on samsguide.living. Run those 80% of the time, rotate the rest in to avoid ad fatigue.

### Metrics to track

For each ad, log:
- Inquiries received (count messages mentioning the ad)
- Inquiries that booked (conversion rate)
- Average booking value from that ad
- Repeat rate of customers acquired from that ad

The winning ad isn't necessarily the one with most inquiries — it's the one with highest revenue/THB spent.

### Where else to test these (beyond samsguide.living)

- **Reddit** (r/Bangkok, r/ThailandTourism, r/singapore) — use ANGLE 11 (insider rec) tone, never the polished sales angles
- **Singapore expat forums** — ANGLE 6 (weekend package) and ANGLE 2 (frequent flyer)
- **HardwareZone EDMW** — ANGLE 11 (insider) carefully, this forum smells marketing fast
- **TripAdvisor Bangkok forum** — ANGLE 12 (educational) and ANGLE 4 (layover)
- **LinkedIn (your founder account)** — ANGLE 2 (frequent flyer) and ANGLE 6 (weekend package)
- **Hotel concierge cards** — ANGLE 3 (discreet professional) printed as small cards

### Important: Channel-fit matters more than copy quality

A great ad in the wrong channel beats a mediocre ad everywhere. samsguide.living readers respond to honest, slightly forum-native tone. LinkedIn readers respond to professional/executive framing. Reddit responds to non-promotional sharing.

Adapt the angle, never just copy-paste across channels.
