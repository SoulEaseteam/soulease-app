## 2. Target personas — 7 segments

### 2.1 Chinese mainland (中国大陆)
- **Channels:** 小红书 (Xiaohongshu/RED), 抖音 (Douyin), WeChat, Trip.com, Mafengwo, Ctrip
- **Decision drivers:** KOL/达人 reviews, hygienic standards, photogenic interiors, "网红" moments, ability to pay via Alipay/WeChat Pay
- **Language need:** Simplified Chinese — staff Mandarin หรือมี translation card / WeChat mini-app
- **Price band:** mid-premium, sensitive to overcharging tourists
- **Pain points:** ภาษา, การจองล่วงหน้า, การชำระเงิน, ความสะอาด

### 2.2 Hong Kong / Taiwan (香港 / 台灣)
- **Channels:** Instagram, Facebook, Klook, KKday, IG bloggers (HK), Dcard (TW)
- **Decision drivers:** Value for money, traditional Thai techniques, location near MTR-equivalent (BTS/MRT)
- **Language need:** Traditional Chinese + English
- **Price band:** premium-friendly, expect transparent menu

### 2.3 Korean (한국)
- **Channels:** Naver Blog, Instagram, KakaoTalk channel, Naver Place, MyRealTrip
- **Decision drivers:** Detailed blog reviews, before/after photos, package combos with hotel/airport transfer
- **Language need:** Korean menu + WiFi password card
- **Price band:** premium, willing to pay for "K-quality" service

### 2.4 Singaporean (新加坡)
- **Channels:** Klook, TripAdvisor, Instagram, SGAG, HardwareZone, lifestyle blogs
- **Decision drivers:** English service, hygiene certification, easy booking, SGD pricing reference
- **Language need:** English (native-comfortable)
- **Price band:** mid-premium, value-conscious but not cheap

### 2.5 Malaysian (Malaysia)
- **Channels:** Facebook (very active), Klook, TikTok MY, lifestyle FB groups
- **Decision drivers:** Halal options, family-friendly, group packages, weekend getaway from KL
- **Language need:** English + Bahasa-friendly staff (signs)
- **Price band:** mid, very promo-driven

### 2.6 Indonesian (Indonesia)
- **Channels:** Instagram, TikTok ID, Traveloka, Tiket.com, Klook
- **Decision drivers:** Halal-friendly menu, prayer time awareness, IG-photogenic setting
- **Language need:** English + Bahasa Indonesia signage
- **Price band:** mid, large group bookings

### 2.7 Vietnamese (Việt Nam)
- **Channels:** Facebook, Instagram, Traveloka VN, Klook, Vietravel
- **Decision drivers:** Affordable luxury, scenic photos, group/couple packages
- **Language need:** English + Vietnamese basic phrases
- **Price band:** mid

## 5. 10 Upgrade pillars — สิ่งที่ต้องลงมือทำ

### Pillar 1 — Brand & positioning
- กำหนด tagline 3 ภาษา: TH / EN / CN เช่น *"Bangkok's Modern Heritage Spa"*
- Brand book: logo lockup, color palette, typography (TH + CN/JP/KR fonts)
- Visual identity ต้องดู IG-worthy แต่ไม่เด็กเกินไป

### Pillar 2 — SEO & web ranking
- Bilingual website (TH + EN เริ่มก่อน, ตามด้วย CN/JP/KR)
- Schema markup: `LocalBusiness`, `Spa`, `Service`, `Review`, `FAQ`
- Target keywords:
  - "spa Bangkok", "best Thai massage Bangkok", "曼谷 SPA 推薦", "방콕 마사지", "バンコク スパ"
- Page speed < 2s (LCP), mobile-first
- Backlink plan: tourism blogs, local directories, partner hotels
- Generative SEO: structured FAQ + "best spa in Bangkok" content

### Pillar 3 — Social media
| Channel | Owner segment | Content cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | All | 4 posts + 5 reels/week, 7 stories/day |
| 小红书 RED | CN | 3 posts/week, KOL collab x2/month |
| Douyin / TikTok | CN/SEA | 3 short videos/week |
| Naver Blog | KR | 1 long blog/week |
| Facebook | MY/ID/VN | 4 posts/week, FB Live monthly |
| LINE OA | TH/JP/TW | broadcast 2x/month |
| WeChat Official | CN | 2 articles/month |

### Pillar 4 — OTA & Google Maps
- เปิด listing: Klook, KKday, Trip.com, Traveloka, MyRealTrip, GetYourGuide
- Google Business Profile: ภาพใหม่ 30 รูป, attributes ครบ, Q&A ตอบทุกข้อ, posts รายสัปดาห์
- Review velocity plan: หลังบริการเสร็จ ส่ง QR ขอรีวิว 3 ภาษา
- Trip.com priority partner program

### Pillar 5 — Service & customer UX
- Multilingual greeting card 7 ภาษา ที่ reception
- Therapist training: phrasebook 50 ประโยค (CN/KR/EN)
- Halal-friendly room option (cleansed, alcohol-free oil)
- Prayer time awareness sheet สำหรับลูกค้ามุสลิม
- Photo-zone: signature corner ถ่ายรูปได้สวย โพสต์ลง IG/RED
- WiFi password card หลายภาษา

### Pillar 6 — Multi-language content
- Menu PDF + physical: TH / EN / 简中 / 한국어 / 日本語 / Bahasa / Tiếng Việt
- Website auto-translate vs. manual: priority CN/EN/KR manual; rest auto with QA
- Video subtitles ทุกตัว
- Booking confirmation email 3 ภาษา

### Pillar 7 — Influencer / KOL
- Tier framework:
  - **Mega (1M+):** 1 ครั้ง/ไตรมาส (CN: 小红书 KOL)
  - **Macro (100k–1M):** 2 ครั้ง/เดือน (KR/TW/HK lifestyle bloggers)
  - **Micro (10k–100k):** 8 ครั้ง/เดือน (mixed segments)
  - **Nano staff/customer UGC:** ongoing
- Tracking: unique promo code ต่อ KOL
- Budget split: 40% CN, 20% KR, 15% TW/HK, 25% rest

### Pillar 8 — Pricing & promotion
- Tiered menu:
  - Express 60 min — entry
  - Signature 120 min — hero
  - Royal 180 min — premium
- Currency-aware display: THB / CNY / KRW / SGD / MYR / IDR
- First-time foreigner promo: 15% off via OTA voucher
- Pre-book online discount 10% to push direct bookings
- Group package: 4+ people 20% off (MY/ID/VN driver)

### Pillar 9 — Loyalty & retention
- "Sunred Sun Club" tiers: Gold / Ruby / Onyx
- Earn 1 point per 100 THB; redeem on next visit
- Birthday voucher (auto via LINE/WeChat/email)
- Refer-a-friend: 200 THB credit each side
- WhatsApp/LINE/WeChat broadcast lists tagged by segment

### Pillar 10 — Partnerships
- Hotel concierge program: 5★ hotel partners — 15% commission, branded brochure
- Airline lounges: Thai Airways / Bangkok Airways co-promo for Asian routes
- BTS/MRT proximity: shuttle from selected station
- Tour operators: Chinese-speaking, Korean-speaking inbound DMCs
- Credit cards: Alipay/WeChat Pay, KakaoPay, GrabPay, KBank lifestyle cards

## 7. Risks & dependencies

- **Negative review crisis:** monitor RED/Naver/Google in real-time; designate Mandarin/Korean responder.
- **Therapist supply:** language-trained therapists are scarce — plan recruiting & retention bonus.
- **Compliance:** Thai spa license, foreign worker permit, hygiene standards (SHA+).
- **Currency volatility:** quarterly pricing review for THB peg vs. CNY/KRW.
