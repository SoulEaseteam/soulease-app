# SunRed Telegram Channel — Post Templates

> Ready-to-use templates for the @SunRed_BKK channel. Rotate to drive
> demand across the entire roster, not just Yuri.
>
> **Brand voice rule:** quiet luxury · concierge tone · euphemisms ·
> restrained emoji (max 1 per post) · use `✦` or em dash for accents.
>
> **How to use:**
> 1. Pick a template category below
> 2. Fill placeholders `[name]` `[area]` `[time]` etc.
> 3. Attach 1-2 photos of the therapist(s)
> 4. Post — DM auto-routes to admin
>
> Last updated: 2026-06-07

---

## ⚠️ Posting principles

1. **Only post when supply is real** — don't promise what isn't there
2. **Rotate therapists** — never feature Yuri 3 posts in a row
3. **Spotlight the lesser-booked** more often than Yuri (build their book)
4. **Time-anchor everything** — "tonight" / "tomorrow" / "this weekend"
5. **Single CTA** — one Telegram DM link · don't overwhelm
6. **Match language to target** — post EN for most, rotate 中 / 한 / 日 / ไทย for specific therapists/segments

## 🎨 Photo guideline (for attached image)

- **Light:** soft, warm (not fluorescent)
- **Frame:** torso-up or full body · no face-cutoff
- **Outfit:** elegant casual (white shirt, premium loungewear) — not lingerie/swimsuit on channel
- **Background:** plain or hotel-aesthetic blur
- **Avoid:** group photo, dark/grainy, obvious "selfie" angle

Save photos in folders by therapist for easy rotation.

---

## 🚫 What NOT to post

- ❌ "Happy ending" / explicit language (loses Telegram channel, brand trust)
- ❌ Prices below standard (devalues brand)
- ❌ Promises beyond supply ("We have 10 therapists tonight" when 2 are ready)
- ❌ Same therapist 3 days in a row (burnout signal + bores subscribers)
- ❌ Discount banners ("FLASH SALE 50% OFF!!!") — anti-brand
- ❌ Walls of hashtags (#bangkok #massage #spa #thai... → 20 tags)

## ✅ Do post

- ✅ Specific names + specific times
- ✅ Real availability only
- ✅ Mix of languages over the week
- ✅ Service variety (not always Gentleman's)
- ✅ 1 strong photo > 5 weak ones
- ✅ Single DM CTA at bottom
