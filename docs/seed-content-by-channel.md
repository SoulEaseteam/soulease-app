# 3. Reddit r/Bangkok soft engagement — 5 templates

Reddit is a long-game · soft engagement only. **Never post a raw
advertisement.** Reply helpfully to actual questions, mentioning
SunRed when DIRECTLY relevant.

⚠️ **Risk:** mods may delete, but readers will DM. Even 1-2
conversions/month = sustainable.

Use a Reddit account that has > 30 day age + some general comment
history. Don't use a fresh account.

## Reddit posting hygiene

- **Wait at least 24 hours** between SunRed mentions across threads
- **Don't reply to your own previous reply** with SunRed mentions
- **Vary the angle** — sometimes price, sometimes language, sometimes
  late-night, sometimes safety
- **If a mod removes your comment** → don't appeal, don't reuse that
  username for a week
- **Track which threads convert** in the Tracking Sheet
  (`docs/acquisition-content-pack.md` §4)
