## 1. 🔴 Stickman Bangkok — week 1

Bangkok's longest-running gentleman-traveller blog/directory.
Real readers, real conversion. View has never tried.

### How to find + pitch

- Website: `stickmanbangkok.com`
- Owner: known as "Stickman" — accepts paid listings + occasional reviews
- Contact: usually via the site's contact form

### First move (30 min)

1. Visit `stickmanbangkok.com/sponsors.html` (or current sponsor page)
2. Note format: usually a banner + short text block
3. Email pitch (English):

```
Subject: Listing inquiry — SunRed BKK premium outcall

Hi,

I run SunRed.vip, a premium outcall massage service in central
Bangkok (Sukhumvit, Silom, Sathorn, Thonglor). We've been
operating since June 2025 with a 12-practitioner roster, 24/7
concierge, and a verified premium positioning.

I'd like to discuss a listing or feature on your site. Could
you share your current rates and format options?

Thanks,
View · sunredbkk@gmail.com · sunred.vip
```

### Expected outcome
- Banner listing or short feature
- Even a small one drives steady traffic during BKK tourist season

## 6. 🟡 Taxi driver referrals — Bangkok-style

Informal but proven. Drivers have customer flow that hotels can't
match (especially 22:00-04:00 window when SunRed prime hours hit).

### How it works in Bangkok

- Cash referral per confirmed booking: ฿200-300
- Driver hands customer your card / Telegram QR
- Customer says "driver sent me" → you confirm + pay driver later

### Action plan

1. Print 100 small premium cards with:
   - Logo
   - Telegram QR
   - "Premium outcall · 24/7 concierge"
   - English + Chinese on back
2. Hand out at:
   - Asok / Nana / Sukhumvit taxi queues during evening
   - Hotel taxi stands (don't engage hotel staff · just driver)
3. Verbal pitch (30 sec): "ฉันเปิดร้านนวดส่ง · ลูกค้าฝรั่ง · ส่งมา
   ได้ ฿200/ครั้ง"

### Cost calc
- 100 cards = ~฿500 print cost
- ฿200 per referral × 10 conversions = ฿2,000 cost
- 10 bookings × ฿2,200 avg = ฿22,000 revenue
- **ROI: 10x · low risk**

### First move (this weekend)

- Order 100 premium cards (Canva → Vistaprint or local print shop)
- Hand out 20 over 1 evening · see if any convert
