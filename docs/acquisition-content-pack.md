## 1. Stickman Bangkok — pitch email

Stickman is the biggest single unlock available. Has a real Bangkok
expat audience that converts. Even a small mention drives traffic.

### Recommended approach
- Read 2-3 of Stickman's recent posts first to match his register
- Don't sound like a marketing email — he hates that
- Lead with what makes SunRed *interesting* to write about, not what
  you want to sell
- Offer a free experience for him (review opportunity)

## 3. Taxi driver referral card

Bangkok-style word-of-mouth. ฿200-300 per booking is industry norm.

### Card design spec

- **Size:** standard business card (85 × 55 mm) or slightly taller
  (90 × 60 mm) for premium feel
- **Stock:** 300-350 gsm matte coated — premium tactile signal
- **Finish:** soft-touch laminate if budget allows (+ ฿1-2/card)
- **Bleed:** 3 mm

### Front (primary side)

```
─────────────────────────────────────
                                     │
              [Sun glyph]            │
                                     │
                SUNRED               │
                BANGKOK              │
                                     │
       Premium outcall massage       │
            24/7 concierge           │
                                     │
                                     │
       [QR code → t.me/             │
        SunRedvip_bkk]               │
                                     │
            sunred.vip               │
                                     │
─────────────────────────────────────
```

- Background: cream `#FAF6F0` or off-white `#FAFBFC`
- Brand sun glyph: brand-red gradient `#FE0944 → #FE7A52`
- "SUNRED" wordmark: Fraunces serif, black
- "BANGKOK" subline: Inter sans, small caps, warm clay `#b85c3c`
- Body text: cool slate `#2a1a14`
- QR: black on white, 18 × 18 mm minimum (scan from arm's length)
- Domain at bottom: Inter sans 8pt

### Back (driver side · also include 中文)

```
─────────────────────────────────────
                                     │
   For Drivers:                      │
   ฿200 per confirmed booking        │
   Send guests to:                   │
   t.me/SunRedvip_bkk                │
   WhatsApp +66 63 435 0987          │
                                     │
   司机:每介绍一位成功预订客人 ¥200    │
   Telegram/WhatsApp 联系             │
                                     │
   ┌──────────────────────┐          │
   │ Driver code: ______  │          │
   │ (For payment record) │          │
   └──────────────────────┘          │
                                     │
─────────────────────────────────────
```

The blank "Driver code" line is for the driver to fill — used when
tracking payments.

### Order spec to give the printer

```
Product: Business card
Quantity: 100 (first batch · test)
Size: 90 × 60 mm
Stock: 350 gsm matte coated
Finish: Soft-touch laminate (optional)
Bleed: 3 mm
Sides: Double-sided color
Files: 2× PDF/X-1a (front + back)
Turnaround: standard (3-5 days)
Budget: ฿500-700 for 100 cards
```

### Where to print in Bangkok
- **Local print shop** in any commercial area — fastest, slightly
  pricier per unit but no shipping
- **Vistaprint TH** — `vistaprint.co.th` — good quality, online
  order, 5-7 day delivery
- **Printcious** — boutique cards if you want extra-premium feel

### Hand-out script (Thai)

When approaching a driver at a taxi queue (Asok / Nana / Sukhumvit /
hotel queues):

> "พี่ครับ ผมเปิดร้านนวดส่งให้ฝรั่ง · ส่งลูกค้าฝรั่งมาให้ผมได้
> ฿200/ครั้ง · มีนามบัตรครับ · ส่งผ่าน Telegram QR ในบัตรนี้ก็ได้
> · ลูกค้าจอง สำเร็จเดี๋ยวแอดมินทักกลับมาเช็ก"

Hand the card. If they look interested · take 30 seconds more:

> "ลูกค้าฝรั่งโรงแรมแถวสุขุมวิท สีลม สาทร อโศก ทองหล่อ ส่งมาให้
> ผมได้ทุกคน · จ่ายโอนทุกครั้ง · มีลูกค้ามาบ่อยขึ้น เดี๋ยวเพิ่ม
> เป็น ฿300"

Then move on. Don't oversell. The card does the work.

### Best places + times to hand out

| Location | Time | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Asok BTS taxi queue | 18:00–22:00 | Hotel-bound foreigners |
| Sukhumvit Soi 11 area | 19:00–01:00 | Nightlife driver flow |
| Sathorn / Silom hotel queues | 17:00–20:00 | Business traveler returnees |
| Don Mueang / Suvarnabhumi taxi staging | mid-day | Tourist arrivals |

Aim: hand out 100 cards over 2-3 evenings. Track conversions in
the Tracking template (Section 4).

## 6. After 7 days — what to look for

| Signal | What it means | Next move |
|---|---|---|
| Stickman replied · open to listing | Strong | Negotiate · ship |
| Stickman replied · not interested | Slight loss | Try directly competing directory |

| Booking question "how did you hear?" comes from 1+ new source | Validated | Investigate · double down |
