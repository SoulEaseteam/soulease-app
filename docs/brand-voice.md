## 1. Brand archetype

**The Concierge** — discreet, knowledgeable, attentive, anticipatory.
Think the head concierge at Aman, Mandarin Oriental, or Six Senses:

- Speaks softly but with authority
- Never recommends — orchestrates
- Never asks "what do you want?" — offers "may I suggest..."
- Privacy is reflexive, not a marketing line
- The room is always quiet around them

**Anti-archetype:** the noisy salesperson, the wellness influencer,
the spa receptionist reading from a script.

## 2. The euphemism table (critical)

Adult-adjacent services exist. Copy must communicate them to
industry-aware customers WITHOUT exposing the gray-area to
casual/mainstream readers.

### Map: reality → public copy

| Reality | NEVER write | Use INSTEAD |
|---|---|---|
| Handjob ending | "happy ending", "HJ", "manual release" | **personalised finishing ritual** · **tension-release work** · **closing release** |
| Body-to-body massage | "B2B", "skin-to-skin", "body slide" | **continuous-contact technique** · **whole-body oil ritual** |
| Nuru massage | "nuru" | **flowing whole-body oil ceremony** · **immersive oil ritual** |
| Pretty / sexy therapist | "hot girls", "beautiful", "young", "sexy", "fit" | **trained female practitioner** · **skilled practitioner** |
| Customer | "customer", "client", "punter" | **guest** |
| Therapist / masseuse | "girl", "lady", "masseuse" | **practitioner** · **therapist** |
| Admin | "admin", "team", "staff" | **concierge** |
| Booking | "booking", "session" | **reservation** (formal noun) · **session** is OK informally |
| Senior / 8+ years | "senior", "experienced", "8+ years", "veteran" | **specialised** · **trained in this discipline** (avoid age) |
| Discount | "discount", "promo", "sale", "cheap" | **complimentary upgrade** · **goodwill gesture** |
| Outcall | "outcall" | **outcall** (industry term — keep) · **delivered to your residence** |

### Test for new copy

Before publishing any line, ask:
1. **Casual reader test:** Would a non-industry reader feel any
   discomfort? If yes → soften.
2. **Industry reader test:** Would a Bangkok regular catch the hint
   immediately? If no → strengthen subtly.
3. **Brand voice test:** Does it sound like a concierge or a
   salesperson? If salesperson → rewrite.

## 3. Sentence patterns we use

### Editorial italic em (signature device)
Pattern: `<plain text>, <italic em accent>.`
- "Bangkok's most discreet outcall massage, *delivered to you.*"
- "A ritual in three *movements*"
- "What you can *expect*"
- "Three sessions, *one ritual.*"
- "At your *service*"

### Eyebrow → Serif title → Sans body
Three-tier hierarchy used everywhere:
```
THE EXPERIENCE                    ← eyebrow (10px, tracked, warm clay)
A ritual in three movements       ← serif title (Fraunces, 22-28px)
Reserve in under a minute...      ← sans body (Inter, 13.5px)
```

### Concierge closing line
Always end with hospitality:
- "Should you require further assistance, our concierge is at your service twenty-four hours a day."
- "Available on request your concierge will confirm at booking."
- "For destinations beyond the central districts, our concierge is
  pleased to provide a private quotation."

### Avoid
- Exclamation marks (!)
- All caps shouting
- "Limited time only!" / "Don't miss out!"
- Em dashes mid-sentence are OK; don't overuse
- "Just" / "simply" / "only" softeners that undermine premium tone

## 6. Multilingual copy register

When translating into 中文 / 日本語 / 한국어 / ไทย, preserve:
- The eyebrow tracking style (uppercase doesn't translate, but
  the visual rhythm should)
- The italic em accent on key word (use italic / 强调 / 강조)
- The euphemism layer — DO NOT translate "tension-release" or
  "continuous-contact" literally. Find the equivalent industry
  euphemism in target language.

### Quick reference (industry euphemisms by language)

| Service | 中文 | 日本語 | 한국어 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gentleman's (HJ) | 男士尊享 / 完整释放 | 紳士向け / 完了の儀式 | 신사 코스 / 완성 의식 |

## 7. Forbidden language list

Never write these in production:
- "Cheap massage"
- "Best deal"
- "Hot girls"
- "Sexy therapist"
- "Happy ending"
- "Adult service"
- Anything with explicit anatomy or sexual acts
- Pricing as "starting from $X" (we use ฿ and "From ฿X")

## 8. Tone calibration examples

### ✅ Right tone
- "Should you require further assistance, our concierge is at your service twenty-four hours a day."
- "Reserved for specialised practitioners limited availability."
- "Performed in your private space unhurried, undivided."

### ❌ Wrong tone (too clinical)
- "Our 8+ year experienced therapists provide deep tissue therapy."
- "We offer 4 services with various durations."

### ❌ Wrong tone (too crude)
- "Hot Thai girls come to your hotel for happy ending massage."
- "Sexy masseuse with body-to-body action available now."

### ❌ Wrong tone (too generic)
- "Welcome to SunRed! Book now and save 20%! Limited time offer!!!"

## 9. When in doubt

Default to **less is more, quieter is louder**.

Premium hospitality whispers. It does not announce.
